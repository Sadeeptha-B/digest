# NNNN — Short decision title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](./NNNN-...md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD _(when the decision was recorded)_

## Context

What forced a choice? The constraints, the problem, the options on the table. Enough that a
future reader who never saw the discussion understands the pressure behind the decision.

## Decision

What we chose, stated plainly.

## Consequences

What this buys us, what it costs us, and what now depends on it. Include the residual caveats —
the places where this decision still relies on careful setup or accepts an intentional tradeoff.

> Keep it short. An ADR is a paragraph or two per section, not an essay. Link to the reference
> docs ([`../architecture.md`](../architecture.md)) and code rather than restating them.
